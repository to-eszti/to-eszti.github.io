Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The Strange Part uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It does not collect, store or share personal data from anyone else.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API and the TikTok Content Posting API. Access tokens for the owner's own accounts are stored privately on the owner's computer and are used only to upload videos. The tool does not read or keep any other data from these accounts.

Use of YouTube data is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
